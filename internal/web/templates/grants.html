Editing changes what a grant reaches without the client authorising again: its token keeps working, and does whatever you leave it doing from its next call onwards. Handing something over is shown to you first, because nobody at the client’s end is asked again.

Revoking cannot be undone. The client loses access on its next call, and getting it back means authorising from scratch — which needs whoever runs the client, not just you.
