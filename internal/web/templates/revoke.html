What stops working

  • The token this client holds stops resolving. Every tool call it makes after that returns an error rather than mail, and it will not recover on its own.
  • Nothing else is touched: your mailboxes stay linked, no mail is changed or deleted, and the rows this grant already wrote to the audit log stay exactly where they are.
  • Getting it back is a new grant, approved on a new consent screen. It cannot be un-revoked, and this one’s history does not come with it.
